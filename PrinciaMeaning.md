# princia

**princia** is a coined word built from *principia*, so its meaning comes from there.

## the root

*principia* is latin for "beginnings", "foundations" or "first principles". it's the plural of *principium*, which comes from *princeps*, meaning "first" or "foremost".

newton used it for his 1687 book *philosophiæ naturalis principia mathematica* ("mathematical principles of natural philosophy"), which set out the laws of motion and gravity that everything else in physics builds on.

## what it means for the project

princia carries the idea of the first principles everything is built on. in a version control system for ai agents, that's the foundational record: every change, every decision and the history behind it, which agents and humans can all trace back to.

git tracks what changed. princia tracks the principles and reasoning underneath.

## one-line pitch

princia, from the latin *principia*: first principles. the foundation every change builds on.
